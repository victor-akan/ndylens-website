Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need any tech skills?+

None. NDYLens is built for photographers, not developers — and we help you set up and import your clients personally.

How does it send WhatsApp messages?+

Through the official WhatsApp Business API, connected to your own studio number — so messages are branded, reliable, and fully compliant. No grey tools.

Is my client data safe?+

Yes. Your clients are yours — never shared, never sold. Access is permission-based and you can export or leave at any time.

What does it cost?+

Plans start at ₦130,000/year (or ₦39,000/quarter), billed yearly or quarterly. WhatsApp message fees are billed by Meta directly and are usually just a few naira per message — you see exactly what you use.

Can I upgrade or cancel later?+

Anytime. Start on Starter and move up as your studio grows, or cancel whenever — no lock-in.
