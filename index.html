Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered.

Is NDYLens only for photographers with a lot of clients?+

NDYLens is built mainly for established solo photographers and small studios with enough clients that keeping track of everyone, their bookings and follow-ups manually starts becoming difficult. You don’t need hundreds of clients to use it, though.

Do I have to enter all my clients myself?+

No. You can import your existing client information instead of starting from scratch. You can upload your client data from a spreadsheet, and NDYLens can also extract relevant information from your existing WhatsApp chat history.

Will NDYLens message my clients for me?+

Yes. You can set up automated WhatsApp messages for things like follow-ups, birthdays, anniversaries, booking reminders and other occasions. You can also choose when you want the message to go out and use your own message or an automated one.

What if I don’t want NDYLens sending messages automatically?+

You can choose how you want your follow-ups to work. You can be reminded when it’s time to reach out and send the message yourself, or set up messages to go out automatically.

What kind of clients can I follow up with?+

Any past client whose information you’ve added to NDYLens. For example, someone you photographed for their birthday could come back for their next birthday, a couple you photographed for their wedding could book you for their anniversary, or a family could return for a maternity, newborn or family shoot.

Do my clients need to use NDYLens too?+

No. Your clients don’t need an NDYLens account. They receive their messages and photos through channels they already use, including WhatsApp.

What happens to my clients after I deliver their pictures?+

You can keep their information and booking history in NDYLens, so they’re not just disappearing into your WhatsApp contacts after the shoot. You can use that information for future follow-ups and bookings.

Is early access free?+

No. Early access is ₦15,600 for six months. Our planned regular price is ₦39,000 for three months, but early-access photographers get 60% off and six months of access.

What happens after my six months?+

You’ll be able to continue using NDYLens on a regular paid plan. We’re using the early-access period to validate the product and pricing, so we’ll communicate the final plans and pricing before your six months ends.

Is my clients’ information safe?+

We take the security and privacy of your client information seriously. NDYLens is built with safeguards to protect the client and booking information you provide.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use NDYLens?+

No. If you can use everyday apps like WhatsApp, you should be able to use NDYLens. You’ll also get guided onboarding to help you get set up.
